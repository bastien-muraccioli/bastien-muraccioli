# Hi there, I'm Bastien Muraccioli 👋 🤖

I am a **PhD Researcher in Robotics** at the [CNRS-AIST Joint Robotics Laboratory (JRL)](https://unit.aist.go.jp/isri/isri-jrl/en/index.html) in Tsukuba, Japan. 
My research focuses on making robots safer, more collaborative, and more intuitive for human environments.

### 🔬 Research Focus
* **Physical Human-Robot Interaction (pHRI) for Humanoids & Manipulators**
* **Theoretical Safety Guarantees for Real-World Robotic Deployments**
* **Hybrid Controllers that combine Learning & Model-based Approaches**
